What we do

Site work for homes, ADUs and land.

  1. ADU and house pads

    Cut, fill and compact a level pad that's ready for the foundation crew.

    Recent: an ADU pad, finished and handed over.

  2. Septic systems

    Septic installs for new homes and ADUs, dug and set by the same crew that grades the site.

    Recent: a basic system with a 500-gallon tank.

  3. Grading and land development

    Rough and finish grading, building sites and access on rural Nevada County land.

    Our main line of work.

  4. Drainage and underground utilities

    Trenching and drainage so water goes where it should, and utilities get to the house.

    Recent: a new custom home in Penn Valley.

  5. Asphalt paving

    Driveway aprons and paving with our own paving machine.

    Recent: the apron for a new permitted home.

  6. Emergency calls

    When a foundation or hillside starts to go, call. We've answered that call before.

    Recent: a home whose foundation started collapsing.
