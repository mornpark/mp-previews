This page explains what JW Enterprise does with the information you give us through this website.

What we collect

When you send the form, we get your name, your phone number, your town, the type of job, any details you type in. If you call or email us, we get what you choose to tell us. We don't ask for payment details on this site.

How we use it

Only to answer your request: to call or text you back about the job, give you an estimate, and do the work you ask for. If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. You can reply STOP to any text to stop getting them.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. Form messages are stored in the system we use to track customer requests, which keeps them for us and doesn't use them for its own marketing. Our website host keeps standard server logs. We share information with others only if the law requires it.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies or tracking tools.

Your browser may send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because this site doesn't track you across websites, it works the same either way.

See, fix or delete your information

To see what we have, correct it, or ask us to delete it, email Jwenterprise66@gmail.com or call (530) 277-3179.

Changes
